Our Mission

Pelican Bay Sailing School Inc. is committed to making the sport of sailing accessible to all members of our community, regardless of age, ability, or economic background. Through expert instruction, hands-on learning, and a focus on safety, we cultivate skilled sailors and responsible stewards of our marine environment.

Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Bonita Springs, Florida, we serve Collier and Lee Counties with programs designed to build confidence, leadership, and a lifelong love of the water.

  • ⚓
    Accessibility — No child is turned away due to financial constraints.
  • ⚓
    Safety First — US Sailing certified instructors and rigorous safety protocols.
  • ⚓
    Environmental Stewardship — Teaching respect and care for our coastal ecosystems.
  • ⚓
    Community — Building connections that last a lifetime.

Our Programs

From first-time sailors to competitive racers, we offer programs for every skill level and age group.

Youth Sailing Academy

Ages 8-17. Summer camps and after-school programs teaching fundamentals of sailing, boat handling, and water safety. Scholarships available.

Learn More →

Adult Learn-to-Sail

Beginner through advanced courses for adults. Weekend and evening classes designed to fit busy schedules. Group and private lessons available.

Learn More →

Adaptive Sailing

Inclusive sailing experiences for individuals with physical and cognitive disabilities. Specially equipped boats and trained volunteers.

Learn More →

Racing & Performance

Advanced clinics, regatta preparation, and competitive racing teams for sailors ready to take their skills to the next level.
