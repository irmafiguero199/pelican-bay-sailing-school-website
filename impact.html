Community Testimonials

Hear from the students, families, and veterans whose lives have been changed by access to sailing.

"My son was shy and struggled with teamwork. After one summer at Pelican Bay Sailing School, he came home confident, responsible, and excited about learning. The scholarship made it possible for us, and we will be forever grateful."

Maria Gonzalez
Parent, Bonita Springs

"As a Navy veteran with a spinal cord injury, I had given up on ever feeling the wind in my sails again. The adaptive sailing program gave me back my freedom. The volunteers and staff treated me with dignity and made the impossible possible."

James Richardson
U.S. Navy Veteran, Naples, FL

"I learned to sail at 52 years old through the adult program. Now my wife and I charter boats in the Keys every winter. It is never too late, and the instructors made me feel capable from day one."

Robert Chen
Adult Program Graduate, Estero, FL

"Our students from the Boys & Girls Club have attended Pelican Bay Sailing School for two years. The program builds leadership, discipline, and self-esteem in ways that traditional classroom settings cannot match."

Angela Brooks
Program Director, Boys & Girls Clubs of SWFL
