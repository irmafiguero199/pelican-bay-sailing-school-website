News & Updates

Stay informed about our programs, events, and community impact

Home / News
15Aug 2025

Fall Registration Now Open

Registration is now open for our Fall Youth Sailing Academy and Adult Weekend Intensive courses. Classes begin September 8, 2025, and run through November 14, 2025. Scholarships are available on a rolling basis for qualifying families. Spaces are limited to ensure low student-to-instructor ratios. View program details →

22Jul 2025

New Boat Donation Expands Fleet

Thanks to a generous local donor, we have added two new 420 racing dinghies to our fleet. These boats will allow us to serve an additional 20 students per session and strengthen our racing program. The boats were officially launched at a community ceremony on July 20, 2025, at Bonita Bay.

10Jun 2025

Summer Camp Sells Out for Second Consecutive Year

Our 2025 Summer Youth Sailing Academy reached full capacity with 48 students enrolled across four two-week sessions. Notably, 40% of enrolled students are attending on full or partial scholarship, demonstrating our commitment to accessibility. Waitlist registration is now open for 2026.

05May 2025

Partnership with Special Olympics Florida Announced

We are proud to announce a formal partnership with Special Olympics Florida to provide training and event hosting for adaptive sailing athletes. This partnership will expand our adaptive program and bring regional Special Olympics sailing events to Bonita Springs beginning in 2026.

18Mar 2025

Community Regatta Draws Record Crowd

Over 200 community members attended our annual Community Regatta on March 15, 2025. The event featured races, demonstrations, food vendors, and a silent auction that raised $8,500 for our scholarship fund. Thank you to all volunteers, sponsors, and attendees.

12Feb 2025

Winter Racing Clinic Concludes Successfully

Our eight-week Winter Racing Clinic concluded on February 28, 2025, with 18 participants advancing their racing skills. Three clinic graduates have been invited to join the Pelican Bay Racing Team for the 2025 spring season.

20Nov 2024

Adaptive Sailing Open House Welcomes 30 New Participants

Our November Adaptive Sailing Open House introduced 30 individuals with disabilities and their families to the joy of sailing. The event was free and included on-shore demonstrations, equipment tours, and trial sails with our certified adaptive instructors.
